# Character-pack provenance

The optional `shadow` and `commander` packs contain AI-generated fan-art
expression sheets based on character portraits supplied for this project.
They are not official artwork and this project is not affiliated with the
franchises or their rights holders.

- `shadow`: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon, Zeta, Eta from The Eminence in Shadow.
- `commander`: Tenka Izumo, Kyouka Uzen, Ren Yamashiro from Chained Soldier / Mato Seihei no Slave.

Sheets were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool in
October 2026, then inspected for atlas dimensions, alpha transparency and
expression layout. Character designs and underlying franchise rights belong
to their respective rights holders. This repository does not assert a general
commercial license to those characters or extend its code's MIT license to
the PNGs. Generated frames can vary in alignment and small clothing details.

The default `neutral` pack contains no franchise artwork or character persona.
Its companion badge is drawn by the renderer's MIT-licensed code. You can use
the core without selecting either fan-art pack. Original supplied portraits,
private generation histories and user configuration are not distributed here.
